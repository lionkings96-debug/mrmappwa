Privacy & Settings

Your device

Private by design

MrMAP stores your entries in this browser on this iPhone. No account or cloud sync is used by this starter version.

Important: browser storage is not encrypted and can be cleared by iOS/Safari. This version does not include automatic backup. Export a copy regularly if you enter important information.
Reminder limitation: on iPhone, a web app cannot reliably fire scheduled reminders while fully closed without web-push infrastructure. MrMAP can show due reminders while open. For dependable alerts, add key appointments to Apple Calendar or Reminders.
